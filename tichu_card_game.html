Tichu Rules & Quick Reference

1. Goal & Scoring

Partnerships (You + North vs. West + East) race to reach the target score (default 1000). Each deck contains 100 card points: 5s = 5 pts, 10s & Kings = 10 pts, Dragon = +25 pts, Phoenix = -25 pts.

Double Victory: If both partners go out 1st and 2nd, the round ends immediately and their team scores +200 pts (card points are ignored). Otherwise, the 4th player's remaining hand goes to the opposite team, and their won tricks go to the 1st-place player.

2. Tichu Calls

  • Grand Tichu (±200 pts): Called after seeing your first 8 cards, claiming you will go out 1st.
  • Small Tichu (±100 pts): Called anytime before you play your very first card, claiming you will go out 1st.

3. The 4 Special Cards

  • 🀄 Mah Jong (1): Lowest card; holder leads the first trick. When played, you may wish for a rank (2–A). Subsequent players must play that rank if they legally can.
  • 🐕 Dog: Can only be led on an empty table. Immediately passes the lead to your partner.
  • 🦅 Phoenix (-25 pts): Beats any single card (except Dragon) by +0.5 rank (1.5 if led), or acts as a wildcard in Pairs, Steps, Trips, Full Houses, and Straights (never Bombs).
  • 🐉 Dragon (+25 pts): Highest single card (beats Ace and Phoenix). Cannot be used in combos. If it wins a trick, you must give that trick to an opponent.

4. Valid Combinations & Bombs

You must match the lead combination type and count with a strictly higher rank: Single, Pair, Consecutive Pairs (Steps: e.g. 44-55-66), Three of a Kind, Full House (3+2), or Straight (5+ cards).

💣 Bombs: Any Four-of-a-Kind or Straight Flush (5+ suited cards) beats any non-bomb and can even be played out of turn!
